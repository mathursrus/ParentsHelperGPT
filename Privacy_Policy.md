**Privacy Policy**

*Last updated: July 3, 2025*

### Introduction

The Mothers/Fathers Helper GPT app helps you with scheduling tasks and calendar management using Google Calendar. This Privacy Policy explains our minimal data usage practices clearly.

### Information We Access

Our app accesses your Google Calendar solely to:

* View your calendar events.
* Add, modify, or delete events based on your instructions.

### Information We Do Not Collect or Store

* We do not store, save, or share any personal data or event details from your Google Calendar.
* We do not collect your email, name, or other personal identifiers for any purposes beyond temporary session-based usage.

### Data Security

Since we do not store or retain any user information, your data remains entirely within your Google Account. We use Google's OAuth authentication to securely access your calendar.

### Third-Party Disclosure

We do not share any user data with third parties. All interactions with your calendar remain private and are managed directly via secure Google services.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this policy. Any updates will be clearly communicated.
